your headlights are dead.
the hydraulics still work.

press any key

WASD drive

S/A/D + tap: pop the back or a side

SHIFT lift the nose   QE three-wheel

follow the marks to the bridge · watch for the exits

every landing lights the dark

find the ones watching. land close while they look back.
